## Project-made assets
- Pixel font `alrect-pixel.woff`: generated by `scripts/make-pixel-font.py` from a 5×7 bitmap table written for this project. Covered by the project's MIT licence.
- Pixel sprites, logo and sound effects are drawn or synthesised in code; no external image or audio files.

## Reminder
Review licences again when you upgrade a dependency. No lockfile exists yet (see INSTALLATION.md), so a fresh install may resolve different transitive versions.
