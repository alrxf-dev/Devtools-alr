# Privacy

- Files you open are processed in your browser. The app has no upload endpoint and sends no file data anywhere.
- No analytics, trackers, ads or third-party requests. The page's Content-Security-Policy only allows its own origin (plus `blob:` for downloads and media preview).
- Local storage is used only for favourites and recent tools, in your browser. Clear it any time from browser settings.
- Sound effects are generated in the browser with the Web Audio API. They start after your first click or key press, as browsers require.
- The Donate page links to Saweria (saweria.co/alzhenjb) and the About credits link to the creator's GitHub, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and email. They open in a new tab or your mail app; the app itself makes no request to them, and each service handles your visit under its own policy.
